Partner Spotlight

GiveLink

In-kind giving with the receipts built in · givelink.app

GiveLink lets your organization post a wishlist of real, verified needs: hygiene kits, school supplies, blankets, whatever your program actually runs on. A donor picks an exact item and buys it. Then comes the part that matters: your team sends back photo proof and a short note, and that donor sees precisely where their generosity went. It is the trust loop that built charity: water, applied to goods and services. And it is free: no fees for the nonprofit, none for the donor.

Why it earns the first spotlight on this page. Consistent stewardship at this level is normally an infrastructure problem. It takes a CRM, a thank-you rhythm, and a person whose whole job is closing the loop. Most small shops have none of those, and no budget to hire them. GiveLink builds the loop into the product itself: the report-back happens as a side effect of using the tool, not as one more task on an overworked ED's list. If your organization does not yet have the staffing for donor stewardship done right, this is a discipline you can install in an afternoon.

One piece of setup advice from our own review of the platform: before your first wishlist item goes up, name one person who owns the photos and thank-you notes. The model runs on kept promises.

We refer GiveLink to organizations that need this muscle before they can afford to build the whole gym. When you are ready for the gym, you know where we are.
